## Scope

V1.20 adds **31 deterministic, recipe-driven material presentations** without renumbering the stable 800-scene/native-ID catalog: ten metal-forming programs, ten architectural-glass fracture programs, ten reinforced-concrete failure programs, and the combined **Apex Materials Proving Ground**. The director now contains **65 heroes**: 34 retained timelines plus these 31 new programs.

- every program owns explicit boundary conditions, load geometry, strike locations, fixture layout, specimen orientation, crack/fracture style, camera track, and timed action beats;
- the Apex presentation is a true three-bay composite of independent metal, glass, and concrete solvers at stable world offsets, with targeted load, unload, dent, and fracture actions rather than a cosmetic scene switch;
- recipe behavior is opt-in, so the 50 native material scenes, ten signature-scene contracts, exact 100-item AAA registry, and source-frozen benchmark identities remain unchanged;
- the combined presentation reserves 3,072 CPU material proxies plus a 288-member GPU segment allowance inside the existing 3,360-instance soft-render arena;
- Poliigon-derived concrete, galvanized-metal, glass, and stone tags extend the shared AAA stage while physical deformation, shards, spall, reinforcement, and debris remain solver-authored;
- automated coverage constructs all 31 derived programs, verifies 10/10/10/1 registry ownership, runs an exact paired three-material replay, and soaks the added programs independently of the stable catalog;
- no new visible-browser capture is claimed for v1.20; retained browser evidence is historical until the current runbook is completed.

V1.19 ships an exact, executable 100-item AAA production pass without changing authoritative rigid/soft/material simulation state or rewriting the source-frozen performance history:

- `AAA_IMPROVEMENTS` contains exactly 100 stable, uniquely numbered records: ten for each of the ten signature scene IDs;
- every record owns one or more finite, positive-scale runtime primitives and the static documentation ledger matches all 100 runtime labels in exact order;
- only the active signature scene stages its authored architecture, forensics, lighting, safety, and story cues; ordinary catalog scenes add no signature primitives;
- signature props share the cached instanced effects/shadow path, reserve identity ahead of transient particles, and are identical under native Box3D WASM and deterministic preview physics;
- maximum-stress material/rope segment streams now have a separate 3,360-instance soft-render reservation, preserving the complete 1,536-instance Ultra effects allowance even with a full 4,096-body rigid stream;
- repeated paused impacts are bounded at 3,072 particles and 32 shockwaves, eliminating unbounded invisible update work;
- keyboard controls ignore editable/interactive targets, key repeats, IME composition, and OS/browser modifiers; global pause freezes both simulation and the cinematic clock;
- the in-app 800-scene sweep constructs and verifies the correct stage stream, reports 100/100 improvements, and restores the active signature stage afterward;
- concrete crater aggregate now selects the mineral/stone atlas tag instead of the former woven-fabric tag.

The current material-realism pass replaces several deliberately cinematic placeholders with physically clearer presentation while retaining deterministic solver authority:

- metal, concrete, and glass skins derive an orthonormal basis and current extents from four deformed lattice corners, eliminating axis-aligned floating tiles and visible patch gaps;
- sheet/glass solver lattices are hidden in cinematic presentation, while concrete exposes only actual reinforcement after cover loss;
- cold forming uses an engaging/retracting press, restrained contact flash, non-emissive coating flakes, and subtle galvanized witness bands instead of welding-scale sparks and glowing strain bars;
- concrete starts without pre-authored rubble or duplicate straight rebar, opens shallow surface fissures, emits graded concrete/mineral fragments, settles debris on the authored catch tray, and renders dust through a low-opacity material path;
- glass and glass shards render in a conditional, two-sided Fresnel pass with alpha blending, depth reads, no depth writes, and no opaque shadow silhouette; alternating backlight bars reveal transmission and grazing response;
- procedural sky/ground specular gives bent galvanized surfaces readable indirect reflections away from the key light;
- Dawn compiles and submits both 1× and 4× versions of the new transparent path with zero uncaptured errors.

The numbered runtime/document contract is [`AAA_100_IMPROVEMENTS.md`](docs/AAA_100_IMPROVEMENTS.md).

## Retained and rejected directions

The largest robust retained groups are native construction, high-count water work, stable wrapper pointer access, qualified cloth work, frame preparation, and renderer configuration paths. Large unchanged-frame and wrapper microcall ratios are work-elimination evidence near the timer floor; they are not multiplied into a claimed frame-rate gain.

Two previously missed directions were explicitly re-qualified against current source:

- SIMD `-O2` and scalar `-Os` compiler modes were rejected because active-workload geometric means missed the gate, despite bit-exact outputs.
- A hybrid renderer scratch cache was rejected because its all-path geometric mean was below parity and unchanged cloth/water staging regressed, despite exact output and a passing Dawn check.

TurboQuant remains remote-only. It compresses bandwidth-constrained snapshots, but its codec overhead makes the tested same-thread/local-Worker path slower than raw transfer.
