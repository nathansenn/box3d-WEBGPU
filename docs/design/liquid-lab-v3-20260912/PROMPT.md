Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity design reference for the existing BOX3D Engineering Studio liquid simulation app.
Primary request: a major refinement of an interactive liquids engineering lab with variable viscosity, real shaped beds, pipes, floodable rivers, rain and wet surfaces.
Composition: landscape desktop app mockup. Warm ivory precise interface, dark blue-green typography, small cyan accents; large 70% width 3D scene at left and a narrow orderly controls inspector at right. Top navigation reads "BOX3D", "Liquid dynamics". Inspector has "Set up", "Material", "Test" tabs. Bottom playback and a slim measured-depth legend.
Main scene: physically convincing six metre stainless steel hydraulic test bench in a quiet concrete laboratory with soft daylight. A winding shallow river inset in the bench: realistic gravel stream bed, low grass-covered banks and small boulders. Clear blue-green moving water follows the channel, and a visibly higher level spills over one low bank onto a connected floodplain. No vertical invisible water walls. Continuous connected blue pump and brushed stainless elbow pipe upstream, credible flanges, bolts, supports; downstream grated collection tray.
Two small scene preview insets below main river scene: (1) rain falling onto a crowned dark asphalt table with actual roadside gutters and shallow reflective puddles; (2) thick amber liquid flowing slowly from a narrow steel nozzle onto a smooth glass tray.
Inspector text: "Floodplain overtopping", "Bank height", "Channel width", "Pump head", "Rain intensity". Visible inspection controls: "Natural", "Depth", "Speed". Color legend from deep blue through turquoise to warm amber. Thoughtful spacing, legible controls, no busy giant charts.
Materials: detailed brushed metal and worn fasteners, pebbles at correct scale, restrained natural vegetation, water refraction and fine surface highlights, shallow wet edges and rain impact rings. Technical product visualization that can guide a real-time Three.js implementation.
Constraints: keep apparatus on a plausible contained lab bench, visible inlet and outlet, honest depth-based scene, no impossible floating pipework, no personnel, no unrelated imagery, no extra marketing slogans.
