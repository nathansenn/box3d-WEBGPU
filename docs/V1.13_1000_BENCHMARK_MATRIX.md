## Measurement and acceptance policy

- Eight timed samples per implementation and case, with equal AB and BA order
  strata and deterministic shuffled case order within each shard.
- Warmup, outer subject construction, deterministic capture, compilation, and
  process startup are outside timed regions. World construction is inside the
  timed operation for the three create protocols.
- Batches target at least 25 ms. All cases below 22.5 ms after their documented
  safety cap are excluded from geometric means. Changed cases receive the
  `timer-floor` final label; controls retain the `control` label so control
  accounting remains complete. The final run contains 85 below-floor changed
  cases and 118 below-floor controls, or 203 excluded cases total.
- Each case uses at least 20,000 deterministic stratified-bootstrap resamples.
- Four trajectory checkpoints plus every timed-sample final capture verify
  authoritative state, matrices, colors, topology, counts, replay hashes,
  finiteness, and path execution as applicable.
- Exact paths must be bit-identical. Approximate qualified cloth paths must be
  deterministic and stay below the established 0.2% state-error ceiling.
- A provisional win requires at least 1.03× point speedup, a lower 95% bound
  above 1.0, and both order strata at or above 0.98×. The merge checker then
  applies global multiplicity and aggregate/control gates.
- Eligible changed paths must retain at least a 1.01× aggregate geometric mean.
  Eligible controls must remain at or above 0.98× globally and within every
  group that contains controls.

### Independent control-confirmation gate

An eligible control with point speed below 0.94× and a one-sided regression
p-value surviving global Benjamini-Hochberg correction at `q <= 0.05` is only
a screen, not an automatic failure. The frozen checker launches **eight fresh
Node processes** for each screened case. Every process runs at least eight
balanced AB/BA samples, targets 25 ms, and has a repetition cap of at least
1,024. The unit of inference is the order-balanced speedup from each independent
process. A case fails only if the Bonferroni familywise one-sided upper
bootstrap bound remains below 0.94×.

No eligible control in the final source-frozen run met **both** screen
conditions—a slowdown of at least 6% and one-sided BH `q <= 0.05`. The release
therefore has zero required confirmation case IDs and generated no confirmation
runs. This does not assert that every individual control point estimate was at
or above 1.0×; it says no multiplicity-screened material regression reached the
fresh-process stage. The prospective gate remains encoded for future runs.

## Renderer and artifact validation bound to the run

The merged evidence rejects stale renderer or native artifacts. The recorded
renderer SHA is also embedded in the required Metal files:

- 4,096 rigid bodies: 327,680 initial bytes, 262,144 transform-only bytes,
  65,536 color-only bytes, and **0 settled unchanged bytes**;
- upload-cache probe: 29.638 ns cached versus 1,004.146 ns changed
  (**33.880×** microcall ratio), with 0 cached bytes;
- zero uncaptured WebGPU errors;
- native build provenance and native/soft artifact hashes match the source
  captured by the tournament.

These checks prove resource, upload, and submission contracts on the measured
Mac/Metal environment. Visible browser QA and physical-GPU frame timing remain
separate release gates.
